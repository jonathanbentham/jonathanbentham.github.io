About Me

Introduction

I’m Jonathan Bentham, a Gameplay Programmer & Technical Designer working in Unreal Engine. I currently design and script missions and build gameplay systems on Ground Branch at MicroProse, and work on TimeSplitters Rewind at weekends. Before that, I did co-development work for studios including SpaceForm Technologies and Youmio.

To understand how I got here, you need to realise how my love for programming and gaming developed.

The first game I ever played was Halo on the Xbox in 2001. Being in control of the protagonist was something I’d never seen, it was like playable cinema. Since then I’ve played FPS games for years, always looking for the most fluid mechanics, intelligent AI and above all, the best gameplay loop.

Undertaking a maths degree is what sparked my passion for programming, which led me to pursue a masters degree in computer science. Here I gained knowledge of object-oriented programming and self-taught Python for my thesis.

To pair the two, I started learning Unreal Engine and C++, and since 2023 I’ve been doing it professionally: shipping missions, multiplayer features, character movement and gameplay systems across PC, mobile and Fortnite (UEFN).
